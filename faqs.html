FAQs

Questions

What is Ship of Theses?

Memos on companies I find interesting — what they are, what the numbers say, and what I think should happen next.

How often does a memo come out?

Roughly one a month. There is no fixed schedule; a memo goes out when it is finished.

Is this investment advice?

No. Everything here is my own opinion, written for interest rather than for anyone to act on. I hold no position in the companies covered unless a memo says otherwise.

Can I listen instead of read?

New memos come with a recording. Where one exists, the player sits at the top of the memo, above the text.

Can I suggest a company?

Yes — submit a company. I read everything that comes in.

How do I get memos by email?

The subscribe box at the foot of the index sends each memo out as it publishes, and nothing else.
